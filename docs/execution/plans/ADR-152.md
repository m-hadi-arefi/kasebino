## 1. Executive Summary

ADR-152 defines the operational boundary for product costs and tax calculations in MerchantOS. ERPNext serves as the financial brain and true accounting System of Record (SoT) for tax schedules, General Ledger (GL) entries, and Item Tax Templates. To give merchants operational margin hints without creating duplicate/conflicting accounting logic in MerchantOS, this plan introduces an optional `costAmountMinor` field on `products`, exposes cost in merchant management APIs/DTOs (while strictly stripping cost from public storefront DTOs for secrecy), and documents ERPNext as the sole owner of GL tax books.

---

## 2. Iranian First UX & Localization Compliance

- **Persian Field Labels (`fa-IR`):** Cost inputs and margin hints ship with Persian labels (`قیمت خرید / قیمت تمام‌شده`, `نمایش به تومان`).
- **Toman Display:** Cost amounts displayed in تومان for presentation (`formatTomanDisplay`) while persisted in IRR minor units (`bigint`).
- **Secrecy & Security:** Cost information is strictly restricted to merchant management roles (`merchant.write` / `merchant.read`) and omitted from public customer-facing storefront DTOs (`publicProductDto`).

## 3. Scope & Technical Changes

### A. Database & Schema
- Update `products` table in `src/infrastructure/database/schema/catalog.ts` with:
  - `cost_amount_minor`: bigint (nullable)
- Generate migration `src/infrastructure/database/migrations/0009_catalog_product_cost.sql`.

### B. Domain & Application
- Update `Product` domain aggregate in `src/modules/catalog/domain/product.ts` to include optional `cost?: Money | null`.
- Update `createProduct` and `updateProduct` use cases in `src/modules/catalog/application/use-cases.ts` to validate `costAmountMinor >= 0` when provided.
- Update repository mappers (`DrizzleProductRepository` & `InMemoryProductRepository`) to map `costAmountMinor`.

### C. HTTP API Surface & DTOs
- Update `productDto` in `src/infrastructure/http/dtos.ts` to include `costAmountMinor` and `costDisplayToman`.
- Ensure `publicProductDto` omits cost details for privacy/security.
- Update `createProductSchema` and `updateProductSchema` in `src/infrastructure/http/handlers/catalog.ts` to accept optional `costAmountMinor`.
