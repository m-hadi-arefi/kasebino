# Business Model

## Value proposition

Merchants pay (directly or indirectly) for increased repeat purchases via customer capture at POS, loyalty, and retention analytics — without hiring marketers or adopting heavy ERP.

## Revenue streams

| Stream | Description | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction fees | Fee on digital payments / online orders | MVP+ |
| Marketing credits | Prepaid credits for SMS / campaigns | MVP+ |
| Premium features | Advanced analytics, automation, paid plans | Post-pilot |
| Advertising network | Sponsored placement across merchant base | Future |

## Unit economics (track from day one)

Platform metrics to instrument before monetization locks:

- DAM / MAM
- GMV
- Revenue
- ARPU
- LTV
- CAC

## Go-to-market (Phase 1)

- Geographic: Kerman local retail
- Channel: Direct merchant onboarding
- Activation: Merchant completes first sale with customer phone within first session
- **Pricing stance (ADR-091):** free pilot for Phase-1 Kerman merchants (no charged SaaS or transaction fee enforced in product). Instrument unit economics before locking paid plans.

## Pricing openness

Exact post-pilot fee percentages and credit packs remain unresolved until after pilot learning. Architecture must support:

- Configurable fee rules per merchant/plan (inactive during free pilot)
- Credit balances for SMS/campaigns
- Feature flags for premium capabilities

Landing Pricing copy for MVP: **پایلوت رایگان کرمان** (not invented fee tables).

## Product ↔ monetization coupling

- Online orders and digital payments are the primary fee surface.
- Loyalty and campaigns drive returning customers → higher GMV → fee/credit revenue.
- Analytics (North Star: Monthly Returning Customers) proves value for retention & upsell.
