## Title

CRM Profiles History Segments Dashboard

## Context

CRM-01..04 and MEM-01..02 are P0. Membership domain exists in-memory only.

## Problem

Merchants cannot view customers, history, or segments; POS capture cannot link durable memberships.

## Current State

crm-membership contracts + module domain; no CRM pages; CRM-04 dashboard stats stub.

## Desired State

Merchant CRM list/profile/history/segments; POS phone capture creates store membership atomically.

## Requirements

- CRM-01..04
- MEM-01..02
- Soft-delete exclusion
- Segment new/returning/lapsed from sales

## Technical Design

Membership aggregate as SoT for store customers; sale completion joins membership; OLTP projections feed CRM-04 widgets.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Profile shows purchase history
- [ ] Segments update from sales without manual rebuild
- [ ] Dashboard customer stats live within 60s TTL

## Risks

- Phone normalization inconsistencies across IR formats

- ADR-007
- ADR-063
